Why it works: the question “where is it from?” is the exact thought a viewer has. The answer arrives as social proof, not an ad.

B · Stitch close-up ASMR

Craft format · 15–25s · no talking, trending soft audio
“Zoom in. Every dot on this shirt is a stitch.”
TimeShotOn-screen text
0–2sFull shirt flat-lay on a wooden table, then fast zoom into one panel.“zoom in.”
2–6sExtreme macro of the honeycomb panel. Light raking from the side to throw shadow on the thread.“every dot is a stitch”
6–12sThree quick macro cuts, one per panel: red, purple, black.“[technique 1] · [technique 2] · [technique 3]”
12–18sArtisan's hands mid-stitch (ask your friend for 5s of workshop footage).“[X] hours. one pair of hands.”
18–22sCut to the café wear shot (the photo setting), smiling.“wearable art. Kuru.”

Why it works: macro texture stops the scroll and makes the value obvious without ever saying the price. Replace each bracket with the real technique names, for example kantha, French knots or appliqué, whatever your friend actually uses.

Positioning

Keep it premium without naming a price

Say

  • Hours and days of handwork, with a real number
  • “Small batches”, “no two the same”, “one pair of hands”
  • Occasions: weddings, festive dinners, brunch, travel
  • “DM for the lookbook” as the only call to action

Avoid

  • Price, “affordable”, discounts or “sale” in the launch posts
  • Replying with prices in public comments; move them to DMs
  • Over-produced studio edits that stop it reading as UGC
  • Heavy filters that hide the thread texture
